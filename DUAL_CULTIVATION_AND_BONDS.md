# Dual Cultivation, Dao Companionship & Lineage Bonds (`DUAL_CULTIVATION_AND_BONDS.md`)

Cooperative cultivation resonance mechanics, Master–Disciple inheritance, and Guild/Alliance pact multipliers in **Voxel Realms RPG (`v1.5.0`)**.

---

## 1. Master–Disciple & Sect Transmission (`src/game/cultivationMath.ts`)

- **Proximity Resonance**: Disciples meditating within range of an elder Master receive accelerated Qi & Mind refinement rates scaled by the Master's realm differential.
- **Spirit Stone Taxation (`5% – 20%`)**: Masters configure Sect tribute rates transparently; collected stones fund communal formation upkeep and disciple protection decals.
- **Skill Scroll Transmission**: Senior cultivators copy mastered techniques onto jade slips without losing their own comprehension (`Four-Path Cultivation.md`).

---

## 2. Dao Companion Bonds & Alliance Pacts (`src/game/clanMath.ts`)

- **Soul Resonance Link**: Paired Dao Companions gain shared elemental Spirit Root harmonization bonuses (`+10%` Yin-Yang breakthrough stability during Heavenly Tribulations).
- **Co-Op World Boss Synchronization**: Bonded allies attacking the `600M HP` Void Leviathan or contested Territory War Sectors (`A1`–`C3`) combine flanking multipliers without suffering friendly-fire AoE attrition.

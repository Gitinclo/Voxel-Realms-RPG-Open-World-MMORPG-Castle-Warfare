# ⚖️ Multiplayer & Progression Balance Philosophy (`BALANCE_PHILOSOPHY.md`)

> **Project:** Voxel Realms RPG (`v1.5.0` · `15/11/2026`) · **Supreme Author:** [`creatergame31@gmail.com`](mailto:creatergame31@gmail.com)

- **Clone PvP Balance**: Combat Clones deal `35%` PvE damage but are capped at **`20%` PvP damage** and `40%` HP so clones never trivialize PvP fights (`avatarCloneShadow.ts`).
- **Authoritative Caps**: Server clamps speed (`<=45 m/s`), rally caps (`<=1T`), and stat deltas (`antiCheat.ts`).

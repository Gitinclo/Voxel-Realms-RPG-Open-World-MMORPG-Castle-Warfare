# Community Guidelines (`COMMUNITY_GUIDELINES.md`)

Standards for respectful collaboration, fair play across open-world MMORPG servers, and constructive contribution across **Voxel Realms RPG (`v1.5.0`)**.

---

## 1. Core Expectations

- **Be Constructive & Inclusive**: Welcome newcomers setting up Three.js, TypeScript, or C++20 builds (`ONBOARDING.md`). Critique designs and code cleanly, never individuals.
- **Fair Play & Anti-Exploit Integrity**: Respect the rules of competitive 50v50 clan sieges (`siegeNetProtocol.ts`). Exploiting packet manipulation, client memory tampering, or harassment violates both these guidelines and `EULA.md`.
- **Lore & Balance Etiquette**: When submitting cultivation balance feedback or lore additions, use `.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/balance_feedback.md` or `lore_inconsistency.md` with reproducible math or epoch citations.

---

## 2. Moderation & Reporting

- All community spaces are governed by `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` and enforced per `.github/CODE_OF_CONDUCT_ENFORCEMENT.md`.
- Report griefing, abuse, or Code of Conduct violations privately to **`creatergame31@gmail.com`** or via the in-game Support Ticket system (`/api/db/tickets`).

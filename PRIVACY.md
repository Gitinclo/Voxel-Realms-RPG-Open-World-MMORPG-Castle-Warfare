# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 23, 2026

Welcome to **Voxel Realms: Kingdom & Cultivation RPG** ("the App", "we", "our", or "us"). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and protect it, when it may be shared with third-party processors, and your legal rights regarding your personal data.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

* **Account & Registration Data:** While basic single-session or local play is supported, if you create a user account, link a profile, or contact us for support, we may collect:
  * Username or display name, email address, and authentication credentials.
  * Cloud-synced gameplay data associated with your account.
* **Local & Cloud Game State Data:** The App generates and stores gameplay progression data, including:
  * Character level, Cultivation Realm status, Karma score, and Master/Disciple hierarchy state.
  * Kingdom resources (Wood, Stone, Gold, Food, Diamonds, Spirit Stones, and Veteran Scrolls).
  * Daily Login Reward streak progress, claim timestamps, and audio preferences (`voxel_rpg_sound_muted`).
* **Payment & Transaction Information:** If you make in-app purchases or subscribe to premium features, transaction records (such as item purchased, timestamp, currency, and transaction ID) are collected. Full payment card details are processed directly by our third-party payment processors and are never stored on our servers.
* **Usage & Advertising Data:** When accessing the App, technical metadata (such as IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers/Advertising IDs, WebGL capabilities, and interaction logs) may be collected automatically by our hosting infrastructure or integrated analytics and advertising SDKs.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information collected to:
* **Provide and maintain the service:** Authenticate your account, synchronize or store local/cloud kingdom and cultivation progression, and deliver daily rewards.
* **Process transactions:** Verify in-app purchases, subscriptions, and virtual currency grants.
* **Serve and measure advertisements:** Deliver contextual or personalized advertisements (where applicable) and prevent ad fraud.
* **Ensure security and fair play:** Detect cheating, unauthorized automation, realm/karma exploits, and unauthorized account access.
* **Communicate with you:** Send important account notifications, security alerts, and responses to support inquiries.

---

## 3. Data Storage, Retention & Right to Delete Data ("Right to be Forgotten")

* **Local Storage:** By default, game save states and user preferences are stored in your browser's local storage (`localStorage`) on your device. You can erase local data at any time via the in-game reset option or by clearing your browser's site data.
* **Account Data Retention:** If you register an account, we retain your account information and cloud save data for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide you with the App's services and comply with legal, tax, or accounting obligations.
* **Right to Delete Data (GDPR / CCPA Compliance):** You have the right to request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated personal data ("Right to be Forgotten"). To request data deletion, access, or export:
  1. Use the in-app account deletion option (if signed in), or
  2. Email us at **[creatergame31@gmail.com](mailto:creatergame31@gmail.com)** with the subject line `"Data Deletion Request"`.
  3. We will verify your identity and permanently purge your personal account records within **30 days** (except where limited transaction records must be retained by law for tax or fraud-prevention purposes).

---

## 4. Data Security Measures

We implement administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, loss, or alteration:
* **Password & Credential Protection:** We use industry-standard encryption and cryptographic hashing algorithms (such as bcrypt/Argon2 or OAuth 2.0 token-based authentication) to protect passwords and authentication credentials. Passwords are never stored in plain text.
* **Encryption in Transit:** All communication between your device and our servers or third-party APIs is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
* While we follow industry best practices, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

---

## 5. Third-Party Sharing (Payment Processors & Ad Networks)

We do not sell your personal contact information to data brokers. However, when payment or advertising features are active, we share necessary technical and transaction data with trusted third-party service providers:

* **Sharing with Payment Processors:** When you purchase virtual goods or subscriptions, your payment data is shared directly with third-party payment processors (such as **Stripe**, **PayPal**, **Google Play Billing**, or **Apple App Store**). These processors collect and process your billing information in accordance with their own privacy policies and PCI-DSS security standards.
* **Third-Party Ad Tracking:** If advertisements are displayed in the App, third-party ad networks (such as **Google AdMob**, **Google AdSense**, **Unity Ads**, or **Meta Audience Network**) may use SDKs, cookies, and tracking pixels to collect your device's Advertising ID (IDFA/AAID), IP address, coarse location, and ad interaction metrics to serve relevant ads and measure campaign performance.

---

## 6. Cookie & Tracking Policy

We and our third-party partners use cookies, `localStorage`, web beacons, and tracking pixels for the following purposes:
* **Strictly Necessary Storage:** Browser `localStorage` and session tokens required to save your game state, daily login streak, audio settings, and authentication session.
* **Analytics & Performance Cookies:** To understand frame rates, WebGL compatibility, and feature usage so we can improve gameplay stability.
* **Advertising Cookies & Pixels:** Used by third-party ad networks to cap ad frequency, prevent click fraud, and deliver interest-based advertisements.

**Managing Cookies & Tracking:** You can configure your web browser to reject cookies or clear `localStorage` at any time, and you can reset or opt out of personalized advertising identifiers in your mobile device's system privacy settings ("Limit Ad Tracking" on iOS or "Opt out of Ads Personalization" on Android).

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we learn that we have inadvertently collected personal data from a child without verifiable parental consent, we will delete that information immediately.
